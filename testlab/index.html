XCast test lab

Three pages built like a typical embedded-player site, with a synthetic test video. Open one, press play, then click the XCast icon.

  1. Embedded cross-origin player

    The player lives in an iframe from another HTTPS site and uses a blob: source. XCast cannot see into it until you allow Scan embedded player from … in the popup. Expect a direct cast: that stream is public.

  2. Hotlink-protected stream

    A blob:-source player with disableremoteplayback. The stream answers 403 unless the request carries this site's Referer, and sends no CORS headers, so the TV cannot fetch it. Expect XCast to ask about a device on your own network, then cast relayed.

  3. Signed, expiring MP4 link

    A plain <video src="//host/file.mp4?s=…&e=…">: protocol-relative address, signature, expiry time, Referer check, Range requests. Expect a relayed cast, with seeking.

Watch the terminal running server.py: it prints who asked for each file and with which Referer.
