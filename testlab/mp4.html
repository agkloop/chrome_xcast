Signed, expiring MP4 link

A plain <video src>, no player library. The address is protocol-relative and carries a signature and an expiry time; the file is only served with this site's Referer. Press play, then click XCast.

What should happen

  1. The popup lists the .mp4 as a FILE stream.
  2. Cast first answers that the video is on a device on your own network. Click Cast from it anyway.
  3. The TV's own fetch gets 403 (no Referer), so XCast relays it (status: “Playing on TV (relayed)”).
  4. Seeking on the TV works: the relay passes Range requests through.
