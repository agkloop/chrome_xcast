Embedded cross-origin player

The player below is another site's page in an iframe (the hls.js demo, playing Big Buck Bunny, CC BY). Press play inside it, then click XCast.

What should happen

  1. The popup finds no video and offers Scan embedded player from hlsjs.video-dev.org.
  2. After you allow it, the popup lists the .m3u8 stream.
  3. Cast plays it directly on the TV (status: “Playing on TV”).
